GrowthOS

AI social media marketing assistant for small business owners who do not have time to figure out Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn, or YouTube.

Owner-firstContent + Leads + Auto-DMsNot analyticsMay 2026

Overview

One-line pitch: Social media marketing without the marketing degree — content created, posts scheduled, leads captured, and DMs answered, even if you have never done any of this before.

Owners answer plain-English questions. GrowthOS creates posts, sets up lead capture, turns on auto-replies, and publishes. Leads show up in one inbox.

Strategic north star: Guided marketing execution — not dashboards, not analytics, not marketing education.

Analytics role: Background only. Owners see a leads inbox and a weekly email. Detailed performance data is optional in Phase 2+.

Who It Is For

Yes — this is for you if:

  • You own a small business (1–25 employees)
  • You use or want social media but feel lost
  • You do not have time to learn funnels, DMs, or schedulers
  • You want customers, not a marketing course

Not for:

  • Marketing agencies or social media managers
  • People who enjoy configuring martech
  • Owners who want analytics as the primary product

MVP Features

  1. Get Started wizard — Business type, goal, offer, photos
  2. AI content creation — Hooks, captions, hashtags; platform-specific versions
  3. Lead pages (automatic) — Book a call · Get a quote · Free guide
  4. DM and comment auto-replies — INFO/PRICE triggers auto-DM with link
  5. Publish and schedule — Instagram + Facebook (M4)
  6. Leads inbox — Default home; call, text, mark booked
  7. AI helper chat — Always-available plain-English help

Not in MVP

  • Analytics dashboards
  • Design editor
  • Image/video generation
  • TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube (phased)
  • HubSpot, paid ads
