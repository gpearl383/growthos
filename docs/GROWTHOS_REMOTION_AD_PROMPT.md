## Prompt

Build a Remotion composition for a 30-second Instagram Reel ad (1080x1920, 9:16 portrait, 30fps, 900 frames total) for an app called GrowthOS.

## What GrowthOS does (use this for all copy)

GrowthOS is an AI-powered social media marketing app built for small business owners — plumbers, hair stylists, personal trainers, chiropractors, real estate agents, anyone who runs their own business.

Core value props:
- AI writes your Instagram and Facebook posts for you in seconds
- Generates images and voiceovers automatically
- Creates a lead capture page (a simple landing page with a contact form)
- Auto-replies to Instagram comments — when someone comments "INFO" or "PRICE", the app automatically DMs them your lead page link
- Tracks leads and shows you analytics
- No tech skills needed. One login. Done.

Tagline: "Post smarter. Capture more leads."
CTA: "Try GrowthOS free at growthos-blond.vercel.app"

Brand colors:
- Primary: #16a34a (emerald green)
- Dark background: #0f172a (slate-900)
- White: #ffffff
- Accent/highlight: #4ade80 (emerald-400, for glows)
- Muted text: #94a3b8 (slate-400)

Font: Use Google Font "Inter" (load via @remotion/google-fonts).
Weights needed: 400, 600, 800.

## Scene Breakdown (30 seconds / 900 frames at 30fps)

### Scene 1 — Hook (frames 0–120, 4 seconds)
Goal: Stop the scroll.

- Full dark background (#0f172a)
- Large centered bold white text slides up from below:
  "Still writing your own Instagram posts?"
- Subtitle fades in below (0.5s delay):
  "There's a smarter way."
- Thin emerald green underline animates under "smarter way" (draws left to right)
- Subtle particle/sparkle effect in background (small white dots fading in/out)

### Scene 2 — Problem (frames 120–270, 5 seconds)
Goal: Agitate the pain.

- Transition: horizontal wipe left to right
- Split into 3 stacked pain-point cards, each sliding in from the right with a 15-frame stagger:
  Card 1: "⏰  Spending hours thinking of what to post"
  Card 2: "💸  Missing leads while you're busy working"
  Card 3: "😩  No time to respond to every comment"
- Each card: rounded corners, dark card background (#1e293b), white text, small emoji icon
- Cards fade out together at end of scene

### Scene 3 — Product Intro (frames 270–450, 6 seconds)
Goal: Introduce GrowthOS.

- Transition: fade through black
- GrowthOS logo text animates in center screen:
  "GrowthOS" — large, bold, white, with a green dot or leaf icon to the left
- Tagline types on character-by-character (typewriter effect):
  "Post smarter. Capture more leads."
- Green glow pulses behind the logo text (radial gradient, #4ade80 at 15% opacity)
- After 2 seconds, logo scales down and moves to top of screen

### Scene 4 — Feature 1: AI Post Creation (frames 450–570, 4 seconds)
Goal: Show the "write for me" magic.

- Transition: slide up
- Phone mockup frame (simple rounded rectangle border in slate-700) fills center of screen
- Inside the phone, a mock "Post Studio" UI appears:
  - Small label: "Platform: Instagram"
  - Text animates in line by line (like it's being generated):
    Hook: "Transform your backyard this summer 🌿"
    Caption: "Our team just finished this outdoor kitchen..."
    Hashtags: "#landscaping #outdoorliving #smallbusiness"
  - A small green "✓ Generated by AI" badge fades in at bottom
- Outside phone, text overlay: "AI writes your posts in seconds"

### Scene 5 — Feature 2: Lead Capture (frames 570–690, 4 seconds)
Goal: Show the lead machine.

- Transition: scale punch (phone shrinks away, new content pops in)
- Show a simplified lead capture page preview:
  - Business name at top: "Green Thumb Landscaping"
  - Offer text: "Get a free estimate for your dream backyard"
  - Simple form fields: Name, Phone, Email
  - Green CTA button: "Get My Free Estimate"
- Animated "notification" pops up from bottom:
  🔔 "New lead: Sarah M. — (555) 867-5309"
- Text overlay: "Your own lead page. Works 24/7."

### Scene 6 — Feature 3: Auto-Replies (frames 690–780, 3 seconds)
Goal: Show the automation magic.

- Transition: slide from right
- Dark screen showing a mock Instagram comment thread:
  Comment from @user123: "INFO 👀"
  Below it, animated reply appears (typing dots → message):
  "Hi! Here's our booking link 👉 [your link]"
  Small label: "Sent automatically by GrowthOS"
- Text overlay: "Auto-reply to comments. Capture leads while you sleep."

### Scene 7 — Social Proof / Stats (frames 780–840, 2 seconds)
Goal: Build credibility fast.

- Transition: fade
- Three stat counters animate up (count from 0):
  "5 min" — setup time
  "3×" — more leads
  "0" — posts written manually
- Each stat in large bold green, label in small white below

### Scene 8 — CTA (frames 840–900, 2 seconds)
Goal: Drive action.

- Transition: fade
- Full emerald green background (#16a34a)
- Large white bold text:
  "Try GrowthOS free"
- URL underneath in slightly smaller white:
  "growthos-blond.vercel.app"
- Subtle white arrow pointing down (Instagram "swipe up" hint)
- GrowthOS wordmark in bottom corner
- Whole screen pulses once (scale 1.0 → 1.02 → 1.0) for emphasis

## Animation Defaults

- All text entrances: translateY from +40px to 0, opacity 0→1, over 20 frames, easing Easing.bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)
- All exits: opacity 1→0 over 10 frames
- Transitions between scenes: use @remotion/transitions with SlideTransition or FadeTransition
- Spring config for punch effects: { stiffness: 200, damping: 20 }
- Never use CSS animations or CSS transitions — use interpolate() and useCurrentFrame() only

---

## Audio

- Add a background music track using <Audio> from @remotion/media
- Use a royalty-free upbeat background music file at public/bg-music.mp3 (placeholder path)
- Set volume to 0.3
- Fade in over first 30 frames, fade out over last 30 frames using interpolate on the volume prop

## Setup Commands

```bash
# Scaffold the project
npx create-video@latest --yes --blank --no-tailwind growthos-reel

# Install dependencies
cd growthos-reel
npm install @remotion/google-fonts @remotion/transitions

# Preview in Remotion Studio
npx remotion studio

# Render final MP4
npx remotion render GrowthOSReel out/growthos-reel.mp4 --codec h264
```
